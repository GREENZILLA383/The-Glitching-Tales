# The Glitching Tales — Full Game Creation Document

## 1. Core Identity of the Game
*The Glitching Tales* is an adventure RPG set across multiple magical regions, each with unique creatures, bosses, and lore. The player is **not** Glitch — the protagonist is a customizable human character whose name, appearance, and combat style are chosen at the start of the game.

The world is structured into major chapters, each with its own environment, guardian, and story progression.

## 2. Player Character System

### Customizable Human Protagonist
The player chooses:
* Name
* Appearance
* Clothing
* Cosmetic style
* Combat skill type

### Skill Types
The player chooses one of three core skills:

**MAGICK**
* Spell-focused
* Elemental attacks
* Time manipulation
* Fire, ice, wind, arcane abilities

**MELEE**
* Close-range combat
* Swords, axes, hammers
* High physical strength and defense

**RANGE**
* Long-distance combat
* Bows, slings, crossbows
* Precision and mobility
* (No guns exist in this world)

### Item Matching System
Items come in three types: **Magick**, **Melee**, **Range**.

If the player's chosen skill matches the item's type, the item's abilities become stronger. **No weakening occurs for mismatched items** — matching only ever helps, never hurts.

### Item Types
* Spells (Magick)
* Weapons (Melee or Range)
* Charms (any type)

**Example — Time Spell:**
* Base effect: slows opponent for 3 seconds
* Magick-boosted effect: slows opponent for 6 seconds

## 3. The Wandering Shopkeeper
A recurring NPC who appears in multiple chapters.

**Traits:** Friendly, chatty, gives hints. Has idle, shop, and goodbye animations.

**Dialogue examples:**
* "Quite nice today, huh."
* "Here's some special stuff for ya."
* "Come again soon."

**Shop functions:**
* Buy spells
* Buy weapons
* Buy charms
* Upgrade items

## 5. Chapter 1 — The Emerald Coast
The player's arrival chapter. The human protagonist appears on the Emerald Coast with no memory of how they got there, and makes their way to **Octo Village**.

### Key NPCs
* **Elder Barnico** — the Elder of Octo Village. Looks far younger than "Elder" implies, but is treated as the wisest figure in the village.
* **Octa** — a young octopus from Octo Village. Barnico decides the player will need help and sends Octa along as a companion.

Barnico tells the player about **Croton, King of Crabs**, who has been testing (and defeating) every challenger who comes near his temple. The player and Octa set out to face him.

### Boss: Croton, the Crab King
Based on the creator's concept art.

**Appearance:** Large crab, crown, staff with a circular top, magick aura.

**Abilities:**
* **Wave Summon** — summons waves that push and damage the player
* **Claw Swipe** — a heavy physical lunge attack

Note: earlier drafts of this document also listed "Time Spell" and "Fire Burst" as Croton abilities — both were mistakes. Time Spell is a player-purchasable Magick spell available from the Wandering Shopkeeper (see Item Matching System above); Croton has no fire ability at all.

Croton is a **guardian, not a villain**. He tests the player's chosen skill type rather than acting as the story's antagonist. Defeating him earns passage onward, toward the Ruined Ocean.

**Creatures:**
* Cthulhu (territorial sea giant, creator's own take)
* Flow Song (floating musical creature)
* Fluff Buggle (small friendly creature)

## 6. Chapter 2 — The Ruined Ocean
A corrupted, sunken sea — once home to an entire underwater kingdom, now a graveyard of it.

### Lore: The Underriver
Long before the present day, an underwater kingdom called **the Underriver** stood here, built from and inhabited by many kinds of sea creatures, ruled by **King Oblongtalo**. Oblongtalo sensed an incoming meteor and ordered a full evacuation. Every citizen escaped in time — except Oblongtalo himself, who stayed behind. The meteor strike destroyed the kingdom, leaving the Ruined Ocean behind.

### Key Events
While crossing the ocean, the player is struck down by a wave that unnervingly resembles a closing hand, and sinks to the ruined kingdom's floor. There, the ghost of King Oblongtalo — crowned, translucent — confronts them: he will not let anyone pass without proving their strength in battle first.

### Boss: The Ghost of King Oblongtalo
Guardian of the Underriver's ruins. Tests the player's strength before allowing passage.

Upon defeat, Oblongtalo declares the player "worthy" and teleports them onward to the **Dunes of Force** (Chapter 3).

## 7. Chapter 3 — Dunes of Force (Sands of Vanished Might)
A desert where numbers vanish and reality becomes unstable.

**Purpose:** A transition chapter leading to the Sky Kingdom.

**Lore:** The desert behaves like a living equation. Time and physics distort. Only powerful beings can survive.

## 8. Chapter 4 — Sky Kingdom
A massive floating kingdom made of cloudstone, sky magic, and drifting islands.

**Structure:**
* Sky Entrance
* Sky Path (platforming)
* Sky Ruins
* Sky Gardens
* Sky Rivers
* Stairway of Clouds
* Two Sky Guards
* Gate to the Sun of Time

**Sky Path — pure movement:**
* Floating platforms
* Wind tunnels
* Cloud bridges
* Sky rings
* Air currents

**Two Sky Guards** — guard the Stairway of Clouds and test the player before allowing access to the Sun of Time:
* **Stratus** — calm, slow, heavy attacks
* **Cirro** — fast, sharp, aggressive

## 9. Chapter 5 — Sun of Time
A dimension inside the sun.

### City Time
A glowing city of clocks, gears, and time energy.

### Time Core
A giant hourglass:
* Top = past
* Bottom = future
* Sand = present

The Time Core is cracked, causing:
* Past leaking into present
* Future glitching into reality
* World instability

### Winwatch — Keeper of Time
A golden gear-being with sun-eyes.

**Personality:** Playful, Dramatic, Curious, Powerful.

**Key cutscene** — Winwatch meets the player:
> **Winwatch:** "So you're the one everyone's been talking about! What abilities do you have? I bet I'm faster than you in a race!"

The player refuses due to urgency. Winwatch insists, then joins the party:
> **Winwatch:** "YES! Time buddy team-up!"

Winwatch becomes a companion until Chapter 6.

## 10. Chapter 6 — Sceptermountion
A snowy mountain filled with:
* Ice village
* Guards
* Snow bears
* Clues about a "clock-vibe creature"
* Great Plateaus (parkour)
* Winwatch's fall
* The Gleam of Light
* The final climb

**Key events:**
* Winwatch slips and falls off a cliff
* The trio believes he is gone
* They continue toward the mysterious light
* The chapter ends at the peak

## 11. Chapter 7 — The Final Battle
At the peak, ancient ruins assemble into a giant clock platform.

### Winwatch Returns
He reveals:
* He never died
* He orchestrated the climb
* He needed the Time Core energy
* He is the final boss

### Final Boss: Winwatch
**Abilities:**
* Clock beams
* Time freezes
* Rewind attacks
* Fast-forward slashes
* Gear storms
* Sun energy

### Ending
Winwatch is defeated. The Time Core stabilizes. The world is saved. *The Glitching Tales* concludes.

## 13. Canon Summary
* The player is a customizable human — **not** Glitch.
* Three skill types exist: Magick, Melee, Range.
* Items match skill types and become stronger when matched. No item ever weakens.
* No guns exist in this world.
* Chapter 1 (Emerald Coast): the player arrives, meets Elder Barnico and Octa in Octo Village, and defeats Croton, King of Crabs — a guardian, not a villain.
* Chapter 2 (Ruined Ocean): the sunken kingdom of the Underriver, ruled by King Oblongtalo, who died protecting his people from a meteor. His ghost tests the player before letting them pass to the Dunes of Force.
* The world progresses: Emerald Coast → Ruined Ocean → Desert → Sky → Sun → Mountain → Final Battle.
* Winwatch is a companion from Chapter 5, then the final boss in Chapter 7.
* The game is built on spells, weapons, charms, creatures, bosses, and platforming.
* All lore above is canon.
