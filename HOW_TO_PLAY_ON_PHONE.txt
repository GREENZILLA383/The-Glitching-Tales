THE GLITCHING TALES - PLAYING ON A PHONE OR TABLET
====================================================

The game detects touch screens by itself and switches to touch controls.

CONTROLS
  Left thumb ........ a joystick appears wherever you touch: move
  Drag on the right . look around
  Sword button ...... attack (hold it to keep attacking)
  Jump button ....... jump; hold it in the air to glide (after Elder Barnico gives you the glider)
  Tap anywhere ...... talk, read, shop, menus
  Pause (top right) . save, settings, fullscreen, quit

BEST WAY #1  -  open the web page and "install" it (needs the game uploaded to a website, e.g. GitHub Pages)
  Android (Chrome): open the page -> menu (three dots) -> "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
  iPhone / iPad (Safari): open the page -> Share button -> "Add to Home Screen".
  The game then opens fullscreen from its own icon and keeps working with no internet after the first visit.

BEST WAY #2  -  one single file (no website needed)
  Copy  glitching-tales-MOBILE.html  to the phone (USB cable, Google Drive, email to yourself...)
  and open it with Chrome (Android).  It works with no internet at all.
  (iPhones don't like opening local files in Safari - use way #1 on an iPhone.)
  Tip: glitching-tales-OFFLINE.html is the very same game; the MOBILE one just forces the touch controls on.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your progress saves automatically (and the moment you switch apps). It is stored on that device.
  - Phones start in "Low Graphics" mode (smoother). Pause menu -> "Low Graphics" turns it off if your phone is fast.
  - Pause menu -> "Touch Controls" forces touch mode on or off (e.g. for a touch-screen laptop). Or add  ?mobile=1  to the web address.
  - Turn the phone sideways (landscape).
  - Your own Flow Song:  put it in the "music" folder as flow-song.mp3 (see music/README.txt).
