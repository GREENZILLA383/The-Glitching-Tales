## Chapter 1: The Arrival
Somewhere in the real world, a virus slipped onto a stranger's computer. It shouldn't have been able to do what it did next — it evolved, line by line rewriting itself into something new: a Glitch. And then, impossibly, the Glitch tore itself loose from the machine and became a person.

The moment it happened, it was pulled out of that world entirely and dropped into one that shouldn't exist: Terosandia.

Sand, warm and endless in every direction. Overhead, the sky shimmered faintly, like static caught behind glass. The Glitch woke up flat on its back staring up at it, and one question surfaced before any other.

"Who... am I? What am I? And most importantly — where am I?"

A voice answered, dry and amused, from somewhere close by. "Not knowing what you are? That's the least of your problems. I'd start worrying about where you are."

The Glitch sat up. Standing over it was an octopus — small, round, tentacles loosely knotted like it had been waiting a while — but its eyes were ancient, and it wore the look of something that had watched this exact scene play out before and still found it funny every time.

"...Who are you?"

The octopus's smirk widened. "I am the Elder of Octo Village. And you, whatever you are, just landed in the one corner of Terosandia currently being watched by a crab with a crown and a temper problem. So. Welcome."
